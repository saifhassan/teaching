# Semester OOP Project — Instructions & Submission

**Course:** Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)  
**Project type:** Group design & implementation (Java, object-oriented design)  
**Assessment weight:** **5 marks** total (course project component)  
**Deadline:** **Thursday, 8 May 2026** (end of day, unless a different time is posted on eLearning)

---

## 1. Team & scope

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Group size** | **Minimum 1**, **maximum 4** students per group. |
| **Deliverable** | One **semester-long OOP project** of your group’s design: a coherent application that applies **classes, objects, inheritance, polymorphism, encapsulation**, and (where appropriate) **interfaces**, **abstract classes**, **collections**, **file or database use**, and **exception handling** as covered in the course. |
| **Topic** | You choose the **domain** (e.g. library, inventory, booking, quiz, campus service) provided it is **realistic**, **implementable** in Java, and **clearly OOP-driven** (not a single script with no structure). |

**Before you code:** Agree on **roles** (who owns which subsystems) so every member has a defined part for the **video** and **GitHub** documentation.

---

## 2. What to build (minimum expectations)

Your README and code should make the following obvious:

- **Purpose** — What problem does the system solve? Who are the users?
- **Core modules** — Main **packages or groups of classes** (e.g. `model`, `service`, `ui` or console menu).
- **Key OOP features** — e.g. separate **entity** classes, **inheritance** or **interfaces** where they fit, **private** fields with accessors, **validation** and **exceptions** where needed.
- **How to run** — JDK version, how to compile/run `main`, and (if used) how to load **SQL** or config.

**Academic integrity:** Code must be **your group’s work**. Cite any **tutorials or snippets** you adapt. Using **AI** to generate large blocks of code without understanding is discouraged; you must be able to **explain** your design in the video and viva.

---

## 3. Submission requirements (all four)

You must complete **all** items below by **8 May 2026** (and any eLearning cut-off time announced there).

### 3.1 Short video demo (YouTube or similar)

- **Length:** **Maximum 5 minutes** (strictly; shorter is fine).
- **Content (cover in order or clearly in sections):**
  1. **Project purpose** — what the software does and for whom.  
  2. **Why this project** — short motivation (problem, learning goals).  
  3. **Main core modules and functionality** — major classes/subsystems and what they do (show the app or diagrams if useful).  
  4. **All group members** must **speak** — each person presents **their** part (design, module, or feature). Split time fairly within the 5 minutes.
- **Host** the video on **YouTube** (unlisted is acceptable) or another platform; you will **link** it in the **README** and in the **zip** README (see below).

---

### 3.2 Public GitHub repository

- **Upload the full project** to a **GitHub** repository (one repo per group is enough).
- **Include:**
  - All **`.java`** (and any **`.sql`**, **`.properties`**, or other needed assets).
  - A **root `README.md`** (Markdown) that includes:
    - **Project title** and one-paragraph **description**  
    - **Group members:** full **name**, **CMS/ID**, and **section** for each member  
    - **Purpose**, **main modules**, and **how to run** (compile/run steps; DB if any)  
    - **Link to the YouTube (or other) demo video**  
    - **URL to your public GitHub repository**    
- Keep the repo **accessible** to the instructor/TAs (public repo, or add them as collaborators if private—follow your instructor’s policy).

---

### 3.3 eLearning: `.zip` submission

- Submit **one** **`.zip`** file on **eLearning** (no RAR-only or cloud-only links as the primary submission unless your instructor says otherwise).
- The **zip** must include:
  - All **source** **`.java`** files (folder structure preserved is recommended).  
  - Any **`.sql`** (or similar) **database scripts** you use.  
  - Same **`README.md`** from github repo in the **root of the zip**.

**Naming suggestion:** `Project_SectionX_Group_LeadName_.zip` .

---

### 3.4 Group member details (consistency)

- **Names, student IDs, and section** must appear in:
  1. **GitHub `README.md`**, and  
  2. **README inside the eLearning `.zip`**, and  
  3. The **video** (each person introduces themselves or their part).

Mismatches between eLearning, GitHub, and video may delay grading.
